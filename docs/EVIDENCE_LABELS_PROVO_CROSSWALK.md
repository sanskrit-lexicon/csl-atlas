# Evidence Labels → PROV-O and TEI — the atlas-side crosswalk

Date: 2026-09-24

Audience: anyone who reuses atlas JSON outside the atlas — an aggregator, an RDF
publisher, a TEI encoder, or a referee asking what the four certainty labels of
[`docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md)
mean in terms of published standards.

The atlas labels every claim `observed`, `derived`, `inferred` or `reviewed`.
Those four tokens are a house vocabulary. This page says, for each of them, what
the equivalent is in the **W3C PROV Ontology** and in **TEI** certainty and
responsibility markup — with one worked example taken from a committed atlas
review report per label — and states plainly the parts that have **no
equivalent** and must not be serialised as if they did.

## Trust Block

- Evidence: `derived` — hand-authored from the cited specifications, the committed
  vocabulary in
  [`docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md)
  and [`docs/REVIEW_REPORTS.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/REVIEW_REPORTS.md),
  the enums in
  [`data/schema/review-report.schema.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/data/schema/review-report.schema.json),
  and the 19 368 review items in the 14 committed reports under
  [`src/data/review/`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/tree/main/src/data/review).
  The worked JSON rows in §2 are verbatim committed data, abridged only where marked.
- Limitations: a documentation crosswalk, not a serializer — no RDF or TEI is
  generated by this repository. PROV-O cannot grade certainty at all (§4.1), and
  three of the six review statuses have no core PROV relation (§4.2). The §5
  census is a snapshot, not a live figure.
- Validation: the label and status enums were read from the committed JSON
  Schema, not from prose; the §5 counts were computed over the committed review
  reports on `main` at `cda2237`; `npm run validate-review-reports` is what keeps
  those files conformant.
- Owner repo: `csl-atlas` for this page (atlas-side worked examples and the
  no-equivalent verdicts); the **normative** standards mapping is owned by
  `csl-standards` — see §0.
- Next use: a publisher exporting atlas JSON to RDF or TEI reads §1 for the
  mapping, §2 for the shape, and §4 before claiming anything the data does not
  support.

## 0. Ownership — where the normative mapping lives

Standards work does not belong in `csl-atlas`
([`docs/BOUNDARY_RULES.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/BOUNDARY_RULES.md),
[`docs/TEI_ONTOLEX_MIGRATION.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/TEI_ONTOLEX_MIGRATION.md)).
The **normative** label → PROV-O → TEI mapping, the `csl:` namespace terms, and
the dataset-envelope mapping to DCMI/VoID/PAV are therefore owned by
[`csl-standards/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-standards/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md)
— and already referenced from atlas code, in the header of
[`scripts/lib/dataset-meta.mjs`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/scripts/lib/dataset-meta.mjs).

This page is the **atlas-side half** of that crosswalk and adds three things the
owning repository cannot supply, because they are facts about atlas data rather
than about the standards:

1. one worked example per label taken from **real committed atlas JSON** (§2);
2. the **no-equivalent list** stated as verdicts a reuser must honour (§4);
3. a **census of how the labels actually occur** in the committed review reports
   (§5) — which is how §4.5 was discovered.

**Sync rule.** The mapping columns in §1 are a non-normative restatement. If the
label set changes (`evidenceLevel` / `reviewStatus` enums in
[`data/schema/review-report.schema.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/data/schema/review-report.schema.json)),
update this page **and**
[`csl-standards/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-standards/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md)
in the same pass; on a conflict between the two, `csl-standards` wins.

## 1. The mapping at a glance

Non-normative restatement of `csl-standards` §A. `@cert`, `@evidence` and `@resp`
are TEI attributes (Guidelines ch. 21).

| Atlas label | Atlas JSON | PROV-O | TEI | No-equivalent caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `observed` | `"evidenceLevel": "observed"` | `prov:wasQuotedFrom` / `prov:hadPrimarySource` the source record; no transforming activity | `cert="high"` `evidence="internal"`, `@resp` → the source | — |
| `derived` | `"evidenceLevel": "derived"` | `prov:wasGeneratedBy` a deterministic `prov:Activity` that `prov:used` and `prov:wasDerivedFrom` the observed entity; activity `prov:wasAssociatedWith` a `prov:SoftwareAgent` | `cert="high"` `evidence="internal"`, `@resp` → the build script | indistinguishable from `inferred` in PROV alone (§4.1) |
| `inferred` | `"evidenceLevel": "inferred"` | structurally identical to `derived` — a heuristic derivation | `cert="low"` (or `"medium"`) `evidence="conjecture"`, `@resp` → the heuristic | the uncertainty exists **only** in `@cert` / `evidenceLevel` (§4.1) |
| `reviewed` | in practice **not** an `evidenceLevel` value — expressed as `reviewStatus: reviewed-ok` / `reviewed-corrected` plus `reviewer` + `reviewedAt` (§4.5) | value `prov:wasAttributedTo` the reviewing agent; correction is a `prov:Revision` (`prov:wasRevisionOf` + `prov:wasInvalidatedBy`) | `cert="high"` `resp="#reviewer"`; a correction → `choice` / `sic` / `corr` | the agent is **not always** a `prov:Person` (§4.6) |

The three vocabularies are complementary, not redundant: PROV-O records the
*generation graph*, TEI `@cert` records the *confidence*, and the atlas
`evidenceLevel` is a one-token join of both.

## 2. One worked envelope per label

All four rows below are real, committed atlas data from
[`src/data/review/`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/tree/main/src/data/review).
Long `machineValue` payloads are abridged with `…`; nothing else is altered.
Each review report **file** wraps its `items` in the standard provenance envelope
(`schemaVersion`, `license`, `licenseUrl`, `generatedAt`, `sourcePath`,
`recordCount`, `queue`, `assumptions`, `warnings`; other datasets add
`generatedBy`, the literal build invocation) — that envelope's own
mapping to PROV-O / DCMI / VoID is
[`csl-standards` §C](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-standards/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md)
and is not restated here.

Turtle fragments below use these prefixes:

```turtle
@prefix prov: <http://www.w3.org/ns/prov#> .
@prefix dct:  <http://purl.org/dc/terms/> .
@prefix xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#> .
@prefix csl:  <https://sanskrit-lexicon.github.io/csl-standards/ns#> .
```

`csl:` is a placeholder namespace declared by `csl-standards`; it does not
resolve yet (§4.7).

### 2.1 `observed`

From [`src/data/review/h5-anomaly-review.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/data/review/h5-anomaly-review.json)
— a known MW/PW correction pair, present in the sources as text, used for
reviewer calibration:

```json
{
  "reviewId": "h5:known-correction:MW-PW:ABoga:",
  "queue": "encoding-ocr",
  "subject": { "kind": "entry", "lemma": "ABoga", "dictionaries": ["MW", "PW"] },
  "sourcePointers": [
    { "dictionary": "MW", "L": "25336", "line": 89826,
      "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/mw/mw.txt#L89826" },
    { "dictionary": "PW", "L": "15112", "line": 57168,
      "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/pw/pw.txt#L57168" }
  ],
  "machineValue": { "sampleClass": "known-correction", "headword": "ABoga", "petDict": "pw", "…": "…" },
  "evidenceLevel": "observed",
  "reviewStatus": "reviewed-ok",
  "reviewer": "codex",
  "reviewedAt": "2026-06-07"
}
```

PROV-O — a quotation, not a derivation; the entity's content comes straight from
the source record:

```turtle
csl:value_h5_ABoga_mw
    a prov:Entity ;
    csl:evidenceLevel "observed" ;
    prov:wasQuotedFrom   csl:record_mw_L25336 ;
    prov:hadPrimarySource csl:record_mw_L25336 .

csl:record_mw_L25336
    a csl:SourceRecord, prov:Entity ;
    csl:dictionary "mw" ;
    dct:source <https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/mw/mw.txt#L89826> .
```

TEI — highest certainty, responsibility assigned to the source witness:

```xml
<quote cert="high" evidence="internal" source="#mw-L25336">ABoga</quote>
```

### 2.2 `derived`

From [`src/data/review/citation-link-pilot-review.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/data/review/citation-link-pilot-review.json)
— an MW citation `RV 1.1.1` resolved to a VedaWeb identifier by a fixed rule:

```json
{
  "reviewId": "citation-link-target:RV:1.1.1",
  "queue": "citation-link-target",
  "subject": { "kind": "citation", "lemma": null, "dictionaries": ["MW"] },
  "sourcePointers": [
    { "dictionary": "MW", "line": 197092,
      "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/mw/mw.txt#L197092" },
    { "dictionary": "VedaWeb RV", "line": null,
      "href": "https://vedaweb.uni-koeln.de/rigveda/view/id/0100101" }
  ],
  "machineValue": {
    "source": "RV", "locus": "1.1.1", "mandala": 1, "hymn": 1, "verse": 1,
    "vedawebId": "0100101", "citationCount": 1, "verdict": "link-proposed"
  },
  "evidenceLevel": "derived",
  "reviewStatus": "needs-review",
  "reviewedValue": null, "reviewer": null, "reviewedAt": null, "note": ""
}
```

PROV-O — a deterministic activity associated with a software agent:

```turtle
csl:value_citationLink_RV_1_1_1
    a prov:Entity ;
    prov:value "0100101" ;
    csl:evidenceLevel "derived" ;
    csl:reviewStatus  "needs-review" ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy csl:act_citationLinkPilotBuild ;
    prov:wasDerivedFrom csl:record_mw_L197092 .

csl:act_citationLinkPilotBuild
    a prov:Activity ;
    prov:used csl:record_mw_L197092 ;
    prov:wasAssociatedWith csl:agent_citationLinkPilotBuild .

csl:agent_citationLinkPilotBuild
    a prov:SoftwareAgent ;
    dct:title "npm run build-citation-link-pilot" .
```

TEI — same certainty as `observed`, but responsibility moves to the algorithm:

```xml
<ref cert="high" evidence="internal" resp="#citation-link-pilot"
     target="https://vedaweb.uni-koeln.de/rigveda/view/id/0100101">RV 1.1.1</ref>
<!-- in the teiHeader -->
<respStmt xml:id="citation-link-pilot">
  <resp>automatic citation resolution from the locus string</resp>
  <name>npm run build-citation-link-pilot</name>
</respStmt>
```

### 2.3 `inferred`

From [`src/data/review/compound-depth-crosscheck-review.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/data/review/compound-depth-crosscheck-review.json)
— the atlas segments a compound into four members; the external model has not
confirmed it:

```json
{
  "reviewId": "compound-depth-crosscheck:abalIyastva",
  "queue": "compound-depth-crosscheck",
  "subject": { "kind": "entry", "lemma": "abalIyastva", "dictionaries": ["MW"] },
  "sourcePointers": [
    { "dictionary": "MW", "line": 39968,
      "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/mw/mw.txt#L39968" },
    { "dictionary": "Dharmamitra ByT5 (unsandhied)", "line": null,
      "href": "https://github.com/dharmamitra/byt5-sanskrit-analyzers" }
  ],
  "machineValue": {
    "surfaceSlp1": "abalIyastva", "atlasSegments": 4, "modelSegments": null,
    "modelSegmentation": [], "delta": null, "recordCount": 1, "verdict": "model-pending"
  },
  "evidenceLevel": "inferred",
  "reviewStatus": "needs-review"
}
```

PROV-O — **structurally identical to §2.2**. The only thing separating this row
from a `derived` one is the literal `csl:evidenceLevel` value; PROV has no
predicate for "this derivation is a guess":

```turtle
csl:value_compoundDepth_abalIyastva
    a prov:Entity ;
    prov:value "4" ;
    csl:evidenceLevel "inferred" ;     # the ONLY marker of uncertainty
    csl:reviewStatus  "needs-review" ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy csl:act_compoundDepthCrosscheck ;
    prov:wasDerivedFrom csl:record_mw_L39968 .
```

TEI — this is where the distinction actually lives:

```xml
<seg type="compound-depth" cert="low" evidence="conjecture" resp="#compound-depth-crosscheck">4</seg>
```

### 2.4 `reviewed`

From [`src/data/review/low-confidence-alignment-review.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/data/review/low-confidence-alignment-review.json)
— an `inferred` cross-dictionary alignment that a reviewer confirmed. Note that
`evidenceLevel` stays `inferred`: `reviewed` is carried by the review fields
(§4.5), and the reviewer here is an agent, not a person (§4.6):

```json
{
  "reviewId": "low-confidence-alignment:kayya",
  "queue": "low-confidence-alignment",
  "subject": { "kind": "alignment", "lemma": "kayya", "dictionaries": ["MW", "PWG", "PWK"] },
  "sourcePointers": [
    { "dictionary": "MW",  "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/mw/mw.txt#L153504" },
    { "dictionary": "PWG", "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/pwg/pwg.txt#L128035" },
    { "dictionary": "PWK", "href": "https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-orig/blob/master/v02/pw/pw.txt#L89715" }
  ],
  "machineValue": { "confidence": "medium", "variants": ["kayya", "ka~yya"] },
  "evidenceLevel": "inferred",
  "reviewStatus": "reviewed-ok",
  "reviewedValue": "same-lemma",
  "reviewer": "Antigravity",
  "reviewedAt": "2026-05-31T07:49:00.000Z",
  "note": "Automated resolution based on nasalization normalization."
}
```

PROV-O — endorsement adds an attribution and a review activity on top of the
machine value; the prior value is **not** invalidated, because nothing was
corrected:

```turtle
csl:value_alignment_kayya
    a prov:Entity ;
    prov:value "same-lemma" ;
    csl:evidenceLevel "inferred" ;
    csl:reviewStatus  "reviewed-ok" ;
    prov:wasGeneratedBy  csl:act_lowConfAlignmentReview ;
    prov:wasAttributedTo csl:agent_Antigravity .

csl:act_lowConfAlignmentReview
    a prov:Activity ;
    prov:used csl:value_alignment_kayya_machine ;
    prov:wasAssociatedWith csl:agent_Antigravity ;
    prov:generatedAtTime "2026-05-31T07:49:00Z"^^xsd:dateTime .

csl:agent_Antigravity
    a prov:SoftwareAgent ;                 # NOT prov:Person — see §4.6
    dct:title "Antigravity" .
```

TEI — confirmation raises `@cert` and reassigns `@resp`; a *correction* would
instead wrap both values:

```xml
<!-- reviewed-ok -->
<form cert="high" resp="#Antigravity">kayya</form>
<!-- reviewed-corrected would be: -->
<choice><sic resp="#machine">…</sic><corr resp="#reviewer">…</corr></choice>
```

## 3. Review statuses

The six-value `reviewStatus` lifecycle (`machine`, `needs-review`, `reviewed-ok`,
`reviewed-corrected`, `blocked`, `deferred`) is defined in
[`docs/REVIEW_REPORTS.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/REVIEW_REPORTS.md)
and mapped, status by status, in
[`csl-standards` §B](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-standards/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md).
It is a second, orthogonal axis: `evidenceLevel` says how a value was *produced*,
`reviewStatus` says how far human checking has *got*.

Only two of the six occur in committed atlas data today — `needs-review` and
`reviewed-ok` (§5). A consumer that meets `machine`, `blocked` or `deferred` is
reading either a newer report or a non-review dataset.

## 4. What does not map — explicit verdicts

These are the places where a faithful export must **withhold** a statement rather
than invent one.

### 4.1 `derived` vs `inferred` — no core PROV relation

Both are derivations produced by a rule. PROV-O models *how* an entity came about,
not *how much* to trust it, so nothing in the core vocabulary separates a
deterministic rule from a heuristic one. **Verdict: no PROV equivalent.** The
distinction survives only in `csl:evidenceLevel` and in TEI `@cert`. An exporter
that drops `evidenceLevel` has silently upgraded every guess in the atlas to a
fact — the exact failure
[`docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md)
exists to prevent.

### 4.2 `needs-review`, `blocked`, `deferred` — annotations, not relations

None of the three names a `prov:Person` or a completed activity: nobody has acted
yet, the action failed to resolve, or the action was postponed. **Verdict: no core
PROV relation**; model them as annotation entities (`skos:note`) and, in TEI, as
`certainty` with `cert="unknown"` plus a status note. Asserting human attribution
for a `needs-review` row would be a false claim about a person.

### 4.3 `assumptions[]`, `warnings[]`, `recordCount`, `schemaVersion`

Envelope fields with no PROV predicate: `assumptions[]` and `warnings[]` are
editorial annotations on the generating activity (`skos:scopeNote` / `skos:note`),
`recordCount` is a dataset statistic (`void:entities`), `schemaVersion` is shape
conformance (`dct:conformsTo`). **Verdict: no PROV equivalent; use DCMI/VoID/SKOS**
per [`csl-standards` §C](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-standards/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABEL_CROSSWALK.md).

### 4.4 Chart trust blocks

The Evidence / Limitations / Validation / Owner / Next-use block required by
[`docs/CHART_TRUST_TEMPLATE.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/CHART_TRUST_TEMPLATE.md)
is a presentation contract about a *page*, not a property of a value. **Verdict: no
equivalent in either standard**; it does not travel with exported data.

### 4.5 `reviewed` is a label the atlas documents but does not emit

`reviewed` is one of the four values the schema allows for `evidenceLevel`, but
**no committed review item uses it** (§5): a reviewed row keeps its production
label (`observed` / `derived` / `inferred`) and records the human layer in
`reviewStatus` + `reviewer` + `reviewedAt`. This matches the "sits on top" wording
of [`docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/docs/EVIDENCE_LABELS.md),
and it means a consumer must **not** filter on `evidenceLevel == "reviewed"` to
find reviewed claims — it would match nothing. Filter on `reviewStatus` instead.

### 4.6 A reviewer is not necessarily a `prov:Person`

Of the 147 reviewed rows in committed data, 137 are signed by a software agent
(`codex` 130, `Antigravity` 7) and 10 by a human (`gasyoun`). The `reviewer` field
is a free-text identifier with no person/agent type beside it. **Verdict: do not
emit `prov:Person` from `reviewer`** — emit `prov:Agent`, and narrow to
`prov:Person` or `prov:SoftwareAgent` only where the identity is independently
known. The same caution applies to TEI `respStmt`.

### 4.7 The `csl:` namespace does not resolve

`csl:evidenceLevel` and `csl:reviewStatus` are terms introduced by
`csl-standards`; the namespace IRI is a placeholder and no ontology document is
published at it yet. **Verdict: usable as a local convention, not yet citable as a
published vocabulary.**

## 5. How the labels actually occur (snapshot, 24-09-2026)

Computed over the 14 committed review reports under
[`src/data/review/`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/tree/main/src/data/review)
at `main` = `cda2237` — **19 368 items**:

| `evidenceLevel` | items | `reviewStatus` | items |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| `observed` | 20 | `needs-review` | 19 221 |
| `derived` | 9 104 | `reviewed-ok` | 147 |
| `inferred` | 10 244 | `machine` | 0 |
| `reviewed` | **0** (§4.5) | `reviewed-corrected` / `blocked` / `deferred` | 0 |

Pairs actually present: `inferred`/`needs-review` 10 127 · `derived`/`needs-review`
9 094 · `inferred`/`reviewed-ok` 117 · `observed`/`reviewed-ok` 20 ·
`derived`/`reviewed-ok` 10.

Two consequences for a reuser: the atlas corpus is dominated by unreviewed
heuristic claims (99.2 % `needs-review`), and `observed` is rare in review
reports because review queues exist precisely for what is *not* directly observed
— it is not a measure of how much of the atlas is source-grounded.
