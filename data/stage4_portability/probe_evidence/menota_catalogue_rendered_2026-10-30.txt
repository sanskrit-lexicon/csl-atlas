Le lingue nordiche

The main corpus consists of all Menota texts apart from charters and runic inscriptions. All texts in the main corpus are based on complete codices (sometimes with lacunas) or on fragments of once complete codices. The texts are displayed on up to three levels of representation: facsimile, diplomatic and normalised. In addition, many offer a complete morphological annotation, i.e. the dictionary entry and grammatical form of each word. Several texts are also accompanied by colour photographs of the manuscript in question, and some by an additional syntactic annotation.

Read more about the corpus here: Intro-Main-corpus.pdf.
