- **H5334 (Claude Code Opus 5 `claude-opus-5`) — etymology component census: nine dictionaries measured on marker, type, position and the shared-lemma European-vs-*vyutpatti* comparison.** New [scripts/build-etymology-census.py](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/scripts/build-etymology-census.py) (`npm run build-etymology-census`) scans all 898,809 `<L>…<LEND>` entries of the csl-orig display text for WIL, PWG, MW72, MW, PWK, AP90, AP, SKD and VCP and writes [data/etymology-census.json](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/data/etymology-census.json) + its `src/data/` mirror: per dictionary the share of entries stating an etymology (WIL 36.6%, PWG 19.5%, MW72 18.1%, AP90 18.1%, VCP 9.3%, AP 9.0%, SKD 6.4%, MW 5.9%, PWK 2.3%), the surface marker each count rests on, the **type** (`root-reference` · `cognate` · `vyutpatti` · `affix` · `nirukta-citation`) and the **position** of the first marker inside the article body (initial / medial / final). Findings the two pieces of prior art could not show: derivation is overwhelmingly entry-**initial** wherever it is stated at all (MW 96% of its 14,168 root-references, MW72 96% of 9,134, PWG 94% of 22,563, Apte 99% of its 6,834 bracket formulae, SKD/VCP 84% of 2,423/4,420 *vyutpatti* formulae), whereas WIL's affix apparatus splits initial/final 7,284/7,126 — an appended decomposition, not a head-of-entry derivation. PWG and PWK state **almost no Indo-European cognates** (11 and 3 entries — Böhtlingk–Roth cite Sanskrit sources, not IE etymons), while MW carries 2,104 and MW72 1,703; and only SKD/VCP state a kāraka + kṛt formula, which no European dictionary reproduces, though MW (1,182) and PWG (2,542) do **cite** Nirukta. On the 4,134 lemmas shared by MW ∩ PWG ∩ SKD ∩ VCP, 268 are etymologised by both traditions, 941 by the European side only, 235 by the indigenous side only and 2,690 by neither; the commonest split is European `root-reference` against indigenous silence (654 lemmas), with 145 lemmas carrying a European root attribution *and* an indigenous *vyutpatti* on the same word. Rendered at [src/tools/etymology-census.md](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/tools/etymology-census.md) (`/tools/etymology-census`) with a trust block, CSV download on every table, and links back to the frozen n=5 [etymology-style witness](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/tools/etymology-style.md) and to `data/etymology-oracle.json`, neither of which it replaces.
