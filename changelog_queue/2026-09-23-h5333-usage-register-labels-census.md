- **Usage and register labels — comparative census across the 45 CDSL dictionaries (H5333, 23-09-2026, OxAlpha `z-ai/glm-5.3-flash`, [#PR](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/pulls)).** The lexicographers-only label had been studied for MW alone; this generalises it: every usage/register label each dictionary writes into `<ab>`, `<lang>` or `<ls>` is extracted, normalised onto one closed vocabulary published as [`data/lexico/usage_register_mapping.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/data/lexico/usage_register_mapping.json), and reported as counts and entry rates in [`src/data/lexico/usage_register_census.json`](https://github.com/sanskrit-lexicon/csl-atlas/blob/main/src/data/lexico/usage_register_census.json) — 62,812 label instances over 1,506,391 entries (41.7/1,000; 3.86 % of entries labelled), 16 dictionaries with labels. Matching is an exact `(tag, token)` pair, not a token: the census refuses the three biggest false positives — `<ab>lit.` = *literally* (1,722 hits), `<ab>ep.` = *epithet* (1,062), `<ab>L.`/`lang:L.` = *Latin* — all published under `excluded` and asserted absent by the validator and tests.
